 Today I cut the 1/4in diameter steel dowel to 2.7in and then used the metal file to grind down the spot on the dowel that I needed for press fiting the flipper bat to the dowel. Then I used the vice press both of the steel dowels into the flipper bats. I Then attempted to press it the pinball launcher rod with the launcher knob but there were no grips big enough to press fit it. Mr. Budzichowski suggested to me that I should heat the rod so that the plastic knob melts around rod. I then melted the knob onto the rod and had a completed launcher.
