Today I cut out the panels on the Shopbot CNC machine. Firstly, Mr. Dubick showed me the steps to cut out the profile cut with the CNC machine by first inserting the file into the Shopbot program and then setting the zero to the bottom left corner of the machine bed. Then he showed me how to preform an "Air cut" (which is when you set the program to cut when the Z height is set 2in above the sheet of ply wood to view the cut and see if it is correct). After seeing the air cut we set the Z height back to true zero and then let the machine cut it out. I came back in my free period to cut the tabs off the panels but found that the wood was warped and the 1/8 inch onshroud bit was not able to cut them all the way through. I then cut all the tabs off and peeled the wood off the machine bed but it caused the wood to strip. I then took all the pieces of the bed and put them in the shed for the day.
