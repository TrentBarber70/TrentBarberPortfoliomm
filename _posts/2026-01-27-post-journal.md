Today I picked up my work from yesterday and started to edit the files in aspire. After asking a classmate about how to properly prepare files to be used in aspire I found that there were 4 opengeometries in my sketch so I went back into fusion and closed the open lines. I then returned to aspire and closed all the open geometries with the close open lines tool. I then used the nesting tool in aspire make the differnt panels closer to each other so that I can used less material. I then created dog bones in my inserts on the baseplate because after talking to Mr. Dubick I found out that the CNC shopbot can't cut straight corners on inside cuts due to the nature of the spindel bit. I then added the dog bones to inserts and created the edgecuts profile cut for the panels and baseplate using the 1/8 inch onshroud bit. Lastly, I created the interior profile cuts and added tabs to the edgecuts profile and sent the file over to the Fab lab google drive in preparation for tommorow.
